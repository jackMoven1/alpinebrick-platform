**Status**: 🔳 Placeholder – Design Phase  
**Owner**: To be assigned (Inventory Engineer)

---

## Overview

The **Inventory Service** manages product stock and reservations:
- Inventory item CRUD
- Stock level tracking
- Inventory reservations (when orders are placed)
- Inventory releases (on order cancellation)
- Low stock alerts & reorder points
- Multi-location/warehouse support (future)

---

## 🎯 Planned Features
